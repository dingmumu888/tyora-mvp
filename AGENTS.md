# TYORA product and brand guidance

## Brand voice

- Treat TYORA as an energetic product-creator community backed by credible manufacturing expertise.
- Community, idea submission, profile, onboarding, and progress moments should feel encouraging, exciting, specific, and action-oriented.
- Manufacturing assessments, sourcing, pricing, payments, privacy, permissions, and legal content must remain calm, precise, and trustworthy.
- Prefer language that helps creators feel their idea is worth sharing and moving forward. Avoid generic dashboard copy.
- Avoid empty hype, exaggerated promises, forced praise, childish urgency, and AI-sounding phrases. Do not declare every idea “great” or “world-changing.”
- A useful voice pattern is: emotional invitation first, concrete next action second, professional reassurance third.

## Product-review reminder

- Proactively tell the user when existing or proposed public-facing copy feels too flat, too corporate, too exaggerated, inconsistent with TYORA, or likely to reduce trust.
- Also flag layouts that hide the emotional invitation, weaken sharing motivation, or make the next action unclear.
- When changing public copy, keep all supported languages aligned: English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese.
