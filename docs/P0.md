# TYORA P0

- Status: Core workflow, user-report safety loop, and 30-day removed-post cleanup completed locally; pending visual QA and deployment approval
- Priority: P0
- Confirmed: 2026-07-31
- Scope: Community publishing, TYORA replies, moderation actions, customer notifications, public creator profiles

## 1. Publish immediately; moderate afterward

Public community posts must appear in the public feed immediately after a signed-in customer publishes them. Remove manual pre-approval from the normal publishing journey.

New public posts enter the **Awaiting TYORA reply** queue. After TYORA replies, they automatically leave that queue and enter **Replied**.

### Main lifecycle

1. Customer publishes a public post.
2. The post appears immediately in the community and on the creator's public profile.
3. The post enters the admin **Awaiting reply** queue.
4. TYORA publishes a reply.
5. The post automatically moves to **Replied** and the customer receives a notification.

## 2. Simplify the admin community workspace

The primary queues are:

- Awaiting reply
- Replied

Returned and removed posts should remain available as a secondary handling log, not as part of the main reply workload.

Each post provides three main actions:

- Reply
- Return for changes
- Remove

The reply screen must use one public free-text field instead of the current multi-field assessment form. Its prompt may remind staff to cover feasibility, materials or process, indicative cost and MOQ, risks, and next steps, but these must not be separate required inputs.

## 3. Return and removal rules

**Return for changes** and **Remove** must both open a required reason field.

### Return for changes

- Hide the post from the public community.
- Notify the customer with the reason.
- Show a **Revise and republish** action in My TYORA.
- After the customer republishes, show it publicly again and return it to **Awaiting reply**.

### Remove

- Remove the post from all public surfaces immediately.
- Notify the customer with the reason.
- Preserve a private removal record and the reason for the customer and authorized TYORA staff.
- Use recoverable soft deletion for 30 days before permanent cleanup to protect against accidental staff actions.

## 4. Customer notifications

My TYORA must notify the customer when:

- TYORA replies to a post.
- A post is returned for changes.
- A post is removed.

Notifications must link to the relevant post or revision flow and clearly explain the action and reason. Private data, internal notes, and staff-only information must never appear in public views.

## 5. Minimum safeguards for immediate publishing

Immediate publishing still requires:

- Verified email
- Completed required profile
- Posting rate limits
- Duplicate and obvious spam-link protection
- A public report action
- One-click staff removal with a required reason

These safeguards replace routine manual pre-approval; they do not reintroduce a hidden approval queue.

## 6. Public creator profile

Clicking a creator's avatar or display name anywhere in posts or comments must open a public creator profile.

Use a stable system-generated public ID in the URL, for example:

`/creator/{publicId}`

Do not require customers to create a second username. Their website display name may change without breaking the profile URL.

### Public profile content

- Avatar
- Website display name
- Country and flag, when supplied by the customer
- Industry and occupation
- Personal bio
- Join date
- Public post count
- Relevant public community totals such as likes and comments
- A list of all currently public posts, with every post opening its detail page

### Avatar viewer

- Desktop: open a larger image in a modal; close by clicking outside or pressing Escape.
- Mobile: open full screen and support pinch-to-zoom.
- Preserve the image aspect ratio and never stretch it.

### Privacy

Never expose email, WhatsApp, authentication data, private Custom inquiries, admin notes, private posts, returned posts, or removed posts.

Only public, currently visible posts appear on another person's profile. Owners continue to see their private handling history inside My TYORA.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new public post appears in the community without staff approval.
- The new post appears in **Awaiting reply**.
- Publishing one TYORA reply moves it to **Replied** and notifies the customer.
- Returning a post hides it, requires a reason, notifies the customer, and supports revision and republication.
- Removing a post hides it, requires a reason, notifies the customer, and preserves a recoverable private record.
- Clicking an avatar or display name opens the correct creator profile.
- Every visible profile post opens its post detail page.
- Avatar enlargement works on desktop and mobile.
- No private customer or staff information is exposed.

## 7. Simplified post editor and image ordering

The create and edit post experiences must use the same fields, layout, and image-management behavior.

### Product type and country

- Rename the existing **Category** field to **Product type**.
- Do not rename it to **Product name**, because the post already has a separate Product name/title field.
- Product type describes the kind of product, for example phone case, charging cable, or charger.
- Product type does not need to become a primary community navigation item.
- Display Product type and Country next to each other in one row to reduce vertical space.
- Product type may use a searchable suggestion list while still allowing a customer to enter a suitable new type.

### Image uploader

- Remove the separate **Add or replace images** button and upload panel.
- Support a maximum of nine images.
- With no images, show one large, obvious **+** upload tile.
- After an image is added, display the next **+** tile immediately after the last image.
- Continue showing the add tile until nine images have been uploaded.
- Hide the add tile when all nine slots are occupied.
- Allow selecting one or multiple files, limited to the remaining available slots.
- Continue using the shared front-end image optimization rules for compression, orientation correction, and clear previews.

### Image controls

- Show a small **×** control on every thumbnail so customers can remove an image at any time.
- Show the current order number on each thumbnail.
- Allow customers to drag any thumbnail to reorder the images, including moving image eight to position one.
- Reordering must support mouse dragging on desktop and long-press/touch dragging on mobile.
- Update all order numbers immediately after a move or deletion.
- Preserve the selected order when the post is saved; the first image becomes the post's primary image.
- Clicking a thumbnail opens a larger preview without changing its order.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- Product type and Country share one row in create and edit flows.
- The image add tile always follows the final occupied slot and disappears at nine images.
- Removing an image immediately frees one upload slot.
- Dragged image order persists after saving and reloading the post.
- Create and edit flows provide identical nine-image behavior.

## 8. Image display, upload performance, and full-screen viewer

### Complete nine-image display

- A nine-image post uses a true 3 × 3 grid on desktop and mobile.
- Every grid cell keeps the same dimensions, but the entire image must remain visible inside it.
- Use contained image fitting rather than cropping important edges.
- Use a consistent, subtle neutral background behind images with different aspect ratios.
- Never stretch an image or distort its aspect ratio.
- The grid must not clip its final row or hide any part of the ninth slot.

### Compress before upload

Image optimization must happen in the customer's browser **before any file is sent to the server**, because server-side compression alone does not improve upload waiting time.

- Correct EXIF orientation before resizing.
- Reduce images whose longest edge exceeds 2048 pixels.
- Encode normal photographic images as WebP at approximately 82% quality.
- Preserve PNG only when transparency is required.
- Target approximately 1.5 MB or less per uploaded image while keeping product details clear.
- Remove unnecessary image metadata.
- Show an immediate local preview while optimization and upload continue.
- Show individual progress and retry state for every image.
- Upload no more than three images concurrently so mobile connections remain responsive.
- Validate and safely optimize again on the server as a fallback, without uploading the original oversized file first.

### Premium full-screen image viewer

- Open images in a portal covering the entire browser viewport.
- Keep the existing dark overlay treatment, allowing the underlying post to remain subtly visible.
- Display the selected image as large as possible within the available screen while preserving its full aspect ratio.
- Do not limit small images to their natural CSS size; allow sensible enlargement while providing a 1:1 option.
- Desktop supports previous/next arrows, keyboard arrow keys, Escape to close, click outside to close, and zoom.
- Mobile supports horizontal swipe, pinch-to-zoom, and drag while zoomed.
- Always show the current position, for example **1 / 9**.
- Preload the previous and next image so navigation feels immediate.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- Selecting nine mixed-aspect-ratio images shows all nine without cropping their content.
- An oversized phone photo is resized and compressed before the network upload begins.
- The customer can see per-image upload progress and retry a failed image.
- Opening an image retains the current dark overlay treatment.
- The opened image uses the available screen instead of appearing as a small thumbnail in the center.
- Desktop and mobile navigation work across all nine images.

## 9. Final publish button clarity

On the final **Submit** step, replace the icon-only button in the lower-right corner with a clearly labelled primary action.

- Chinese label: **确认发布**
- English label: **Confirm and publish**
- Provide equivalent natural wording for every supported public language.
- The button may retain a publish/upload icon, but the icon must accompany the text rather than replace it.
- Use the same clear label on desktop and mobile.
- Keep the button visually prominent and clearly separate from image-upload actions.
- While publishing, show an explicit loading state and prevent duplicate submissions.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- Customers can immediately understand that the button publishes the complete post rather than uploads another image.
- Public and private submissions both use an unambiguous final confirmation label.
- The button remains keyboard accessible and exposes its full action through an accessible name.

## 10. Public disclosure acknowledgement and IP-risk notice

Before a customer publishes a **public** post, TYORA must clearly explain that public disclosure is not confidential and may affect the customer's ability to protect the idea through patents or trade-secret rights.

Do not use an absolute statement such as "copying has nothing to do with TYORA." The notice must explain the risk without attempting to exclude responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded, and TYORA must continue to provide reporting and moderation channels for suspected infringement or misuse.

### Public-post confirmation

- Replace the current multiple public-content consent checkboxes with one concise, required acknowledgement to keep publishing simple.
- The checkbox must be unchecked by default.
- Show the notice immediately above the final public-publish action.
- Do not apply the public-disclosure acknowledgement to private submissions.
- Provide a nearby **Learn about public-post risks** link that opens the fuller explanation without losing the customer's draft.

Recommended Chinese acknowledgement:

> 我理解本帖的标题、文字、图片以及 TYORA 的公开回复将向所有人展示，并可能被搜索、分享、转载、讨论或模仿。公开发布不属于保密披露，并可能影响专利或商业秘密保护。我确认自己有权发布这些内容，并决定继续公开发布。

Recommended supporting warning:

> 如果你的创意尚未申请保护，或包含需要保密的图纸、结构、参数、供应商及成本信息，请选择“私密提交”或暂不发布。

Recommended public action label:

- Chinese: **确认并公开发布**
- English: **Confirm and publish publicly**
- Provide equivalent natural wording for every supported public language.

### Terms and evidence requirements

- State that the creator retains ownership of their content.
- Grant TYORA only the licence needed to host, display, translate, moderate, and operate the service.
- State that a public post does not create a confidentiality obligation between TYORA and the creator.
- Do not guarantee that public content will remain exclusive or cannot be copied by third parties.
- Require the customer to confirm that they have the right to publish the text, images, and designs they submit.
- Maintain a clear infringement-reporting, moderation, and appeal path.
- Record the accepted notice version, timestamp, locale, customer ID, and post ID so consent can be audited.
- Have the final Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and intellectual-property wording reviewed for the launch jurisdictions by qualified counsel.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- A public post cannot be submitted until the acknowledgement is actively checked.
- Switching to private submission removes the public-disclosure checkbox and warning.
- Switching back to public does not silently pre-check the acknowledgement.
- The full risk explanation is accessible without clearing any completed post fields or selected images.
- Every supported language communicates the same material risks; no translation weakens or hides the warning.
- The stored consent record identifies exactly which notice version the customer accepted.

## 11. Consistent signed-in customer avatar in comments

The comment composer currently falls back to text initials or digits even when the signed-in customer has a saved avatar that already appears correctly in the navigation and post-author header.

- Use the same canonical profile-avatar source in the navigation, post header, comment composer, published comments, replies, notifications, and public creator profile.
- Show initials only when the customer has no saved avatar or the image genuinely fails to load.
- Never derive the fallback from an email prefix, phone-like digits, or an internal identifier when a display name is available.
- After a customer changes their avatar, refresh every visible avatar instance without requiring a new login.
- Preserve the circular crop and image aspect ratio on desktop and mobile.
- Provide an accessible name describing whose avatar is shown.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- A signed-in customer with a saved avatar sees that image beside the comment input.
- Publishing a comment shows the same avatar beside the new comment and any replies.
- Reloading the page, changing language, and switching between desktop and mobile do not replace the avatar with initials.
- A customer without an avatar receives a consistent display-name-based fallback in every location.

## 12. Global visual hierarchy and typography system

TYORA currently uses too many near-white surfaces, faint borders, light grey text, and inconsistent font weights. This makes post content, image areas, actions, comment entry, sorting controls, and replies visually merge together and increases reading fatigue.

Treat this as one global design-system pass rather than a one-page patch. Apply the same hierarchy to public community feeds, post details, publishing and editing flows, My TYORA, public creator profiles, Source, and the main admin workspaces.

### Surface hierarchy

- Use a subtle cool-grey page background, with primary content cards remaining white.
- Give major cards a clearly visible but restrained border and a light shadow.
- Use spacing, background tone, and dividers together; do not put a heavy border around every small element.
- Give image galleries a subtly tinted neutral background so they are distinct from surrounding text.
- Separate the description, question chips, action bar, comment composer, sort controls, and comment list with consistent vertical spacing and dividers.
- Present the comment composer as its own clearly bounded section.
- Keep verified TYORA replies visually distinctive with the existing restrained teal treatment.
- Preserve the same hierarchy on desktop and mobile.

Recommended starting tokens:

- Page background: `#F4F6F9`
- Card background: `#FFFFFF`
- Main border: `#D5DCE6`
- Internal divider: `#E3E8EF`
- Primary title text: `#0B1220`
- Body text: `#253247`
- Secondary text: `#667085`

### Multilingual font family

- Use **Reddit Sans Variable** for Latin text, numbers, interface controls, and English content.
- Use **Noto Sans SC / Source Han Sans SC** for Simplified Chinese.
- Use `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, and `Segoe UI` as system fallbacks.
- Keep TYORA's own logo artwork and brand identity; do not use Reddit Display for the TYORA logo.
- Audit every supported language and provide a suitable fallback instead of allowing browser-dependent mismatched glyphs.
- Self-host production WOFF2 font assets and load only the required language subsets or variable-font ranges to protect page performance.

Recommended font stack:

`"Reddit Sans", "Noto Sans SC", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", "Segoe UI", sans-serif`

### Weight and readability rules

- Page and post titles: weight 700.
- Section headings: weight 600 to 650.
- Navigation, labels, tabs, and buttons: weight 600.
- Body copy: weight 450 to 500.
- Metadata and timestamps: weight 400, using the secondary text colour.
- Do not use weight 300 for public body text.
- Avoid weight 800 or 900 for normal Chinese headings because it reduces clarity and feels visually heavy.
- Do not compensate for weak hierarchy by making every element bold.
- Maintain comfortable line height and minimum readable sizes on mobile.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- A first-time viewer can immediately distinguish the post card, gallery, description, actions, comment composer, sorting controls, and replies without relying on colour alone.
- Titles and body text remain clearly readable in normal daylight and on typical mobile displays.
- English, Chinese, and every supported public language have consistent visual weight and do not unexpectedly fall back to thin glyphs.
- Font loading does not introduce a major layout shift or block meaningful content rendering.
- Pages remain visually structured when images, comments, or TYORA replies are absent.
- The updated tokens and typography are defined centrally and reused instead of being independently hard-coded on each page.

## 13. Community feed hierarchy

Apply the global hierarchy system to the public community list page shown after entering Ideas, not only to individual post-detail pages.

- Keep the page background subtly cooler and darker than the white content surfaces.
- Make the main feed column, navigation/filter bar, post cards, left category navigation, and right-side information modules visually distinct through consistent borders, spacing, surface tones, and restrained shadows.
- Strengthen post titles and primary information while keeping metadata secondary but readable.
- Separate the helpful-count rail, post content, metadata/action row, and thumbnail area without adding heavy boxes around every small element.
- Keep the right-side cards consistent in radius, border, shadow, heading weight, and internal spacing.
- Ensure the layout still feels structured when there is only one post or substantial empty space.
- Apply equivalent hierarchy on mobile, where side modules are collapsed or moved below the feed.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- A first-time visitor can immediately distinguish navigation, filters, the post feed, individual post cards, and supporting sidebar modules.
- The feed does not visually merge into the page background on standard desktop or mobile displays.
- Post cards remain easy to scan without making the interface feel heavy or crowded.
- The community list and post-detail pages clearly belong to the same TYORA design system.
