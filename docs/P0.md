# TYORA P0

- Status: Core workflow, user-report safety loop, and 30-day removed-post cleanup completed locally; pending visual QA and deployment approval
- Priority: P0
- Confirmed: 2026-07-31
- Scope: Community publishing, TYORA replies, moderation actions, customer notifications, public creator profiles

## 1. Publish immediately; moderate afterward

Public community posts must appear in the public feed immediately after a signed-in customer publishes them. Remove manual pre-approval from the normal publishing journey.

New public posts enter the **Awaiting TYORA reply** queue. After TYORA replies, they automatically leave that queue and enter **Replied**.

### Main lifecycle

1. Customer publishes a public post.
2. The post appears immediately in the community and on the creator's public profile.
3. The post enters the admin **Awaiting reply** queue.
4. TYORA publishes a reply.
5. The post automatically moves to **Replied** and the customer receives a notification.

## 2. Simplify the admin community workspace

The primary queues are:

- Awaiting reply
- Replied

Returned and removed posts should remain available as a secondary handling log, not as part of the main reply workload.

Each post provides three main actions:

- Reply
- Return for changes
- Remove

The reply screen must use one public free-text field instead of the current multi-field assessment form. Its prompt may remind staff to cover feasibility, materials or process, indicative cost and MOQ, risks, and next steps, but these must not be separate required inputs.

## 3. Return and removal rules

**Return for changes** and **Remove** must both open a required reason field.

### Return for changes

- Hide the post from the public community.
- Notify the customer with the reason.
- Show a **Revise and republish** action in My TYORA.
- After the customer republishes, show it publicly again and return it to **Awaiting reply**.

### Remove

- Remove the post from all public surfaces immediately.
- Notify the customer with the reason.
- Preserve a private removal record and the reason for the customer and authorized TYORA staff.
- Use recoverable soft deletion for 30 days before permanent cleanup to protect against accidental staff actions.

## 4. Customer notifications

My TYORA must notify the customer when:

- TYORA replies to a post.
- A post is returned for changes.
- A post is removed.

Notifications must link to the relevant post or revision flow and clearly explain the action and reason. Private data, internal notes, and staff-only information must never appear in public views.

## 5. Minimum safeguards for immediate publishing

Immediate publishing still requires:

- Verified email
- Completed required profile
- Posting rate limits
- Duplicate and obvious spam-link protection
- A public report action
- One-click staff removal with a required reason

These safeguards replace routine manual pre-approval; they do not reintroduce a hidden approval queue.

## 6. Public creator profile

Clicking a creator's avatar or display name anywhere in posts or comments must open a public creator profile.

Use a stable system-generated public ID in the URL, for example:

`/creator/{publicId}`

Do not require customers to create a second username. Their website display name may change without breaking the profile URL.

### Public profile content

- Avatar
- Website display name
- Country and flag, when supplied by the customer
- Industry and occupation
- Personal bio
- Join date
- Public post count
- Relevant public community totals such as likes and comments
- A list of all currently public posts, with every post opening its detail page

### Avatar viewer

- Desktop: open a larger image in a modal; close by clicking outside or pressing Escape.
- Mobile: open full screen and support pinch-to-zoom.
- Preserve the image aspect ratio and never stretch it.

### Privacy

Never expose email, WhatsApp, authentication data, private Custom inquiries, admin notes, private posts, returned posts, or removed posts.

Only public, currently visible posts appear on another person's profile. Owners continue to see their private handling history inside My TYORA.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new public post appears in the community without staff approval.
- The new post appears in **Awaiting reply**.
- Publishing one TYORA reply moves it to **Replied** and notifies the customer.
- Returning a post hides it, requires a reason, notifies the customer, and supports revision and republication.
- Removing a post hides it, requires a reason, notifies the customer, and preserves a recoverable private record.
- Clicking an avatar or display name opens the correct creator profile.
- Every visible profile post opens its post detail page.
- Avatar enlargement works on desktop and mobile.
- No private customer or staff information is exposed.

## 7. Simplified post editor and image ordering

The create and edit post experiences must use the same fields, layout, and image-management behavior.

### Product type and country

- Rename the existing **Category** field to **Product type**.
- Do not rename it to **Product name**, because the post already has a separate Product name/title field.
- Product type describes the kind of product, for example phone case, charging cable, or charger.
- Product type does not need to become a primary community navigation item.
- Display Product type and Country next to each other in one row to reduce vertical space.
- Product type may use a searchable suggestion list while still allowing a customer to enter a suitable new type.

### Image uploader

- Remove the separate **Add or replace images** button and upload panel.
- Support a maximum of nine images.
- With no images, show one large, obvious **+** upload tile.
- After an image is added, display the next **+** tile immediately after the last image.
- Continue showing the add tile until nine images have been uploaded.
- Hide the add tile when all nine slots are occupied.
- Allow selecting one or multiple files, limited to the remaining available slots.
- Continue using the shared front-end image optimization rules for compression, orientation correction, and clear previews.

### Image controls

- Show a small **×** control on every thumbnail so customers can remove an image at any time.
- Show the current order number on each thumbnail.
- Allow customers to drag any thumbnail to reorder the images, including moving image eight to position one.
- Reordering must support mouse dragging on desktop and long-press/touch dragging on mobile.
- Update all order numbers immediately after a move or deletion.
- Preserve the selected order when the post is saved; the first image becomes the post's primary image.
- Clicking a thumbnail opens a larger preview without changing its order.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- Product type and Country share one row in create and edit flows.
- The image add tile always follows the final occupied slot and disappears at nine images.
- Removing an image immediately frees one upload slot.
- Dragged image order persists after saving and reloading the post.
- Create and edit flows provide identical nine-image behavior.

## 8. Image display, upload performance, and full-screen viewer

### Complete nine-image display

- A nine-image post uses a true 3 × 3 grid on desktop and mobile.
- Every grid cell keeps the same dimensions, but the entire image must remain visible inside it.
- Use contained image fitting rather than cropping important edges.
- Use a consistent, subtle neutral background behind images with different aspect ratios.
- Never stretch an image or distort its aspect ratio.
- The grid must not clip its final row or hide any part of the ninth slot.

### Compress before upload

Image optimization must happen in the customer's browser **before any file is sent to the server**, because server-side compression alone does not improve upload waiting time.

- Correct EXIF orientation before resizing.
- Reduce images whose longest edge exceeds 2048 pixels.
- Encode normal photographic images as WebP at approximately 82% quality.
- Preserve PNG only when transparency is required.
- Target approximately 1.5 MB or less per uploaded image while keeping product details clear.
- Remove unnecessary image metadata.
- Show an immediate local preview while optimization and upload continue.
- Show individual progress and retry state for every image.
- Upload no more than three images concurrently so mobile connections remain responsive.
- Validate and safely optimize again on the server as a fallback, without uploading the original oversized file first.

### Premium full-screen image viewer

- Open images in a portal covering the entire browser viewport.
- Keep the existing dark overlay treatment, allowing the underlying post to remain subtly visible.
- Display the selected image as large as possible within the available screen while preserving its full aspect ratio.
- Do not limit small images to their natural CSS size; allow sensible enlargement while providing a 1:1 option.
- Desktop supports previous/next arrows, keyboard arrow keys, Escape to close, click outside to close, and zoom.
- Mobile supports horizontal swipe, pinch-to-zoom, and drag while zoomed.
- Always show the current position, for example **1 / 9**.
- Preload the previous and next image so navigation feels immediate.

### Additional acceptance criteria

- Selecting nine mixed-aspect-ratio images shows all nine without cropping their content.
- An oversized phone photo is resized and compressed before the network upload begins.
- The customer can see per-image upload progress and retry a failed image.
- Opening an image retains the current dark overlay treatment.
- The opened image uses the available screen instead of appearing as a small thumbnail in the center.
- Desktop and mobile navigation work across all nine images.
